About Me

Welcome to my corner of the internet! I'm p3dr0G, the mind and soul behind AstroCartel. Since 2020, I've been crafting cosmic soundscapes that blend retro nostalgia with futuristic vibes. My music takes you on interstellar journeys through nebulas, past distant stars, and into the heart of the galaxy.

Inspired by the golden age of web 1.0 and the endless possibilities of space, I create music that's perfect for late-night coding sessions, cosmic meditation, or just vibing to the universe's rhythm.
