About AstroCartel

Welcome to AstroCartel - a personal space for music reviews, vibes, and discoveries. This blog is all about sharing tracks that hit different, from underground gems to mainstream bangers that deserve recognition.

Inspired by the cyber-vaporwave aesthetic and early web culture, AstroCartel combines retro design with modern music commentary. Every review is personal, honest, and written from the heart.

Whether it's hip-hop, electronic, grime, reggaeton, or house - if it's good music, it belongs here.

Tech Stack

Built with pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no database, no backend. Just clean code and neon aesthetics.

Language support for English, Spanish, and Polish thanks to client-side JavaScript. Each article is hand-written and carefully curated.
