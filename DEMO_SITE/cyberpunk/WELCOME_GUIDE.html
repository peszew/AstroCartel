PHASE 01
UNZIP YOUR FILES 📂

Download the .zip folder from your Etsy orders. Right-click and select "Extract All". You will see a folder containing your index.html, assets, and articles.

PHASE 02
LAUNCH WITH NETLIFY 🚢

We use Netlify Drop for free, professional hosting. It's the fastest way to get your site live on your own domain.

URL: app.netlify.com/drop
  1. Drag your entire extracted folder into the big box on the page.
  2. Wait 10 seconds. Your site is now LIVE!
  3. Claim your site: Click "Sign Up" to create a free account so your site stays online forever.
PHASE 03
CONNECT YOUR DOMAIN 🌐

Want to use www.yourname.com? It takes 2 minutes:

  • In Netlify, go to Domain Settings > Add Custom Domain.
  • Type in the domain you bought (from Namecheap, GoDaddy, etc.).
  • Netlify will give you 4 "Nameservers". Copy them.
  • Paste those into your domain provider's DNS settings. Done!
